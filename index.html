About

I am a Consultant Radiographer and Sonographer working clinically in Brisbane and a Clinical Associate Professor. My research focuses on paediatric ultrasound, workforce education, quality improvement in medical radiations, AI in sonography, and work-integrated learning in allied health.

In my academic capacity I perform sessional lectures and tutoring in paediatric sonography, and also supervise Honours and HDR students. I serve as Associate Editor for the Journal of Medical Radiation Sciences, and on the Editorial Review Board of Sonography.

My full publication and peer review record is indexed at ORCID 0000-0003-1843-1602.

Research

Publications & research themes

🔬

Paediatric appendiceal ultrasound

Prospective and retrospective studies of diagnostic accuracy, secondary sonographic signs, Bayesian modelling, and decision-support frameworks for acute appendicitis in children. Includes survey work on Australasian sonographer practice and criteria.

🧠

Transcranial Doppler & neuroprotection

Cerebral haemodynamic assessment in children undergoing cardiac surgery and veno-arterial ECMO, including pre-operative collateral circulation mapping and aortic flow characterisation in sepsis.

🫘

Renal & urological ultrasound

Diagnostic accuracy of ureteric jet Doppler for vesico-ureteric reflux in children, including systematic review and retrospective cohort work on jet angle and location parameters.

🤖

AI & technology in sonography

Professional and educational implications of artificial intelligence in the sonography workforce, including competency frameworks, curriculum considerations, and integration pathways.

🎓

Workforce, education & WIL

Work-integrated learning in sonography training — barriers, enablers, trainee wellbeing, and comparative perspectives. Sonographer training pathway design and curriculum implementation. First Nations non-attendance in paediatric imaging.

📋

Clinical audits & quality improvement

Inter-hospital transfers and imaging in paediatric abdominal pain; DDH screening referral timeframes; spinal ultrasound utility in neonates; contact shielding discontinuation in paediatric radiography; fungal infection sonography in oncology patients.

Education & Outreach

Teaching & regional workshops

Alongside university teaching, I deliver hands-on paediatric sonography workshops in regional Queensland and Northern NSW — areas where specialist paediatric CPD is otherwise limited. These voluntary events are delivered free to attendees, supported by vendor partners and host health services. ABS 2021 Census occupation data (place of work, LGA) show that in some regions, a single workshop reaches the majority of the local sonographer workforce — and those communities consistently serve First Nations populations well above state and national averages.

Voluntary / self-funded outreach

Voluntary

Delivered outside of my primary employment hours, at personal time cost, with travel and logistics sometimes supported by vendor partners (Canon Medical, GE Healthcare, Philips) or professional associations. No remuneration received.
